# Assassin rework — staged gameplay module

Status: **not installed / no skill slots replaced**. Current live version remains 0.13.13.

The nine approved ideas are implemented as a standalone C++17 gameplay core with an explicit host interface. This is not a working in-game release: no native adapter, private table IDs, animation assets, tooltip registration or live hooks have been installed. Slot selection comes before that integration.

## Formula convention

L is effective skill level. `base + growth * L` is used literally (e.g. Omnislash at level 20: 700% weapon damage, 9 hits, +600% AR). Levels are bounded to 1000. Weapon percentages are coefficients passed to the native hit adapter; the adapter must not apply them twice.

## Skills

### Expose Weakness
Shadow Disciplines / Passive

Successful hits against full-life enemies always critically strike. Critical damage bonus: 10 + L percentage points. Native Critical Strike / Deadly Strike must not multiply the same hit twice.

### Omnislash
Martial Arts / Attack

Randomly jump between nearby enemies, allowing repeated targets. 5 + floor(L/5) hits; 200 + 25L% weapon damage per hit; +200 + 20L% attack rating; 10-second cooldown.

Provisional test tuning (not user-finalized):
- 8-yard search radius; one jump every 5 frames; traversable landing positions required.

### Queen of Blades
Traps / Reserved buff

Every second, launch blades at nearby enemies. Each blade: 30 + 5L% weapon damage; +100 + 10L% attack rating. Intended replacement: Blade Shield, not assigned yet.

Provisional test tuning (not user-finalized):
- 15 + 3L mana before reservation efficiency; 8-yard range; up to 3 + floor(L/10) targets, capped at 8.

### ATMG Sentry
Traps / Sentry

Fire 8 shots per second. Each shot: 10 + L% weapon damage; +100 + 10L% attack rating. Successful hits can trigger on-hit effects.

Provisional test tuning (not user-finalized):
- 8-yard range; 80 shots per sentry; up to 5 sentries. Overclock consumes the shot budget faster.

### Spiked Ground
Traps / Reserved buff

Movement leaves overlapping spike patches. Each patch lasts 3 seconds and hits once per second for 25 + 5L% weapon damage; +100 + 10L% attack rating. Patches stack independently.

Provisional test tuning (not user-finalized):
- 15 + 3L mana before efficiency; one patch per 0.2 seconds while moving; 1-yard patch radius; first hit after 1 second.

### Afterimage
Shadow Disciplines / Reserved buff

Successful primary attacks leave an afterimage that strikes the same target after a short delay for 30 + 3L% weapon damage. Afterimages can trigger on-hit effects but cannot spawn further afterimages.

Provisional test tuning (not user-finalized):
- 15 + 3L mana before efficiency; 6-frame delay (0.24 seconds); inherits the initiating attack-rating bonus.

### Death Mark
Shadow Disciplines / Reserved buff

Primary successful hits mark a target for 5 seconds. Store the actual damage dealt by the owner and their owned attacks; release 100% of the stored damage as a physical area explosion on expiry or earlier death. Later hits do not refresh the timer. Death Mark explosions do not feed other Death Marks.

Provisional test tuning (not user-finalized):
- 15 + 3L mana before efficiency; 6-yard explosion radius. Marked targets stop collecting when the buff is disabled.

### Gravity Trap
Traps / Trap

Periodically pull enemies toward the trap and slow enemies inside its area. Bosses and movement-immune enemies keep their native movement restrictions.

Provisional test tuning (not user-finalized):
- 8-second lifetime; 6-yard radius; pull once per second; movement slow 20 + L%, capped at 60%; strongest overlapping slow wins; up to 5 traps.

### Overclock
Traps / Reserved buff

Increase sentry firing speed, consuming available shots faster. Does not accelerate Queen of Blades or Spiked Ground beyond their one-second damage cadence.

Provisional test tuning (not user-finalized):
- 15 + 3L mana before efficiency; +25 + 2L% sentry firing rate.

## Integration contract

- Initialize/reset the engine before accepting any casts in a session. Feed every server frame; rollback/new game clears queues. Resolve units by current typed ID and room, never retained pointers.
- Bind effective skill levels and primary resolved-hit events only after skill slots have been agreed. Expose Weakness must also be applied in the primary hit path, using the provided helpers.
- `Host::hit` performs actual native hit checks, resistance/block handling, weapon snapshots and item/skill proc dispatch. It must preserve secondary lineage through delayed native callbacks. Primary skills may proc effects; proc callbacks cannot recursively create Afterimages or seed Death Marks.
- Do not route proc damage through `onHit` as primary. Engine-owned secondary damage contributes to already marked targets; proc-generated damage and Death Mark explosions do not. Death Mark is physical and uses a separate area-hit route (no weapon coefficient).
- `Host::reserve` performs an atomic budget check and transient mana reservation. Call reconcile when gear, efficiency or available mana changes. A false result must leave the old reservation untouched. Existing AuraBuffs ownership should be used by the future adapter, not a second competing max-mana system.
- Trap and patch centers must be legal map coordinates; use the same-game/same-area hostile-unit filter. Despawn visuals and pets through the verified native cleanup path. Gravity slow combines by strongest effect, not multiplication, and cannot bypass native movement immunity.
- All damaging requests have allowOnHit=true. The adapter must choose the appropriate native attack/missile event and avoid creating fake successful hits on misses. No eight-per-second extra scan loop: ATMG uses the existing frame tick with a fractional accumulator.
- Timers are at 25 Hz: ATMG exactly 8 shots/second before Overclock, Queen once/second, each spike patch once/second. Overclock affects ATMG only in this prototype. Native-trap support is a later binding decision.
- Missing targets cancel Afterimages. Omnislash can hit one target repeatedly and cancels when no target exists; invalid jumps never hit through walls. Resource pools are bounded and expired work is removed.

## Validation

Run `test.cmd`. It compiles only `engine_test.cpp` into `build/assassin-preview`; it never builds or installs the live DLL. Sixteen scenario groups cover formulas, full-life critical checks, repeat targeting, invalid landing, reservations, exact attack cadence, stacked patches, recursion, mark timing/early death, gravity lifetime and cleanup. Native rendering, actual proc compatibility and gameplay remain untested until integration.
