Astral Atlas and item/monster fixes
=================================

The Atlas grants one point per character level through 120, displays three digits, accepts 120-point allocations and supports required levels through 120. The seven-bit requirement field already holds this range. Reset restores the full level budget.

All 56 available modifier clones of the 57 balanced Labyrinth monster types now inherit the corrected Hell resistances (ordinary counterpart plus 15 percentage points). The stale clone values of 200 in every resistance are removed.

All 92 Mythical definitions have a Horadric sky beam in their dropped-item model only, with hardKillOnDestroy enabled. Character/equipment model assets are unchanged.

The eight Splash Charm upgrades toward Collin's Destruction consume one ordinary key instead of the Gem Bag. Gem-credit gates and debits are removed; all other ingredients are preserved. Only the upgraded charm is produced.
