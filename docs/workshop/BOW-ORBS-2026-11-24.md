# Bow skills and crafting orbs — 2026-11-24

Multiple Shot uses Jab's flat physical damage curve, Split Arrow uses Pounce's,
and Strafe uses Fend's. Each gains 12% physical damage per hard point in each of
the other two bow skills, on both its weapon damage and flat physical component.
Strafe retains its Magic Arrow magic-damage synergy, now also 12%.
The three mana-cost curves are halved through native fixed-point mana scaling.

Successful Strafe arrows and bolts splash 50% of their finalized projectile damage
to living hostile secondary targets within four yards. The primary target is
excluded; blocked/missed hits and splash descendants cannot trigger another splash.
Targets retain their own resistance calculations. Split descendants and returning
Multiple Shot arrows inherit their original projectile's damage without reapplying flat bonuses.

All seven ordinary crafting orbs and the seven Trial crafting orbs now use the
existing ground-pickup deposit retry. Manual withdrawals remain in inventory.
Ordinary orbs retain their existing Gems-tab sockets; Trial orbs retain Materials.

Trial crafting orbs only drop through the Trial material branch. Global Herald
and Odious Collector drops no longer include them; Akara no longer sells them.
Blank Trial entry seals, their world drops and recipes remain unchanged.
