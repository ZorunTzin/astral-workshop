# Balance update: Goblins, Bow Mastery and Astral tree

- Goblins: world-area roll 10% -> 30%; Trial-wave roll 1% -> 3%. Existing scheduling, area eligibility and per-session limits remain.
- Bow Mastery: fire/cold/lightning minimum and maximum damage, including their caps, doubled. Dodge/Avoid/Evade each grant 5% at level 1, +1 percentage point per additional level, capped at 35%. Bonuses remain bound to the active bow/crossbow stance.
- Deathfire: three +10 Life on Hit ranks become +2 each; final +20 rank becomes +5. Total: 11. Corpse Explosion remains.
- Hexblade becomes Bloodthirster: 50% chance to cause Bleeding, replacing the Plague proc.
- Blood Drinker becomes Berserker: +1 to Rage (All Classes), replacing life steal. Rage uses stable skill ID 128 (internal name Shield Mastery); resolved damage scaling now honors the oskill on all player classes.
- Node positions and saved allocation bits remain unchanged. Reapply the Astral tree to update an existing inscribed Sigil.

Validated with the combat suite, all-class Rage damage/kill-buff checks, Bow stance scaling/cap/cleanup checks, Goblin probability tests, minted-Sigil payload assertions and data/tooltip audits. Manual in-game validation remains pending.
