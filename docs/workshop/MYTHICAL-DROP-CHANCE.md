Mythical Uniques in Trials
=========================

The independent Mythical roll has weight 1 and NoDrop 1999: 1/2000 (0.05%) at players 1 per native Trial loot roll. The temporary 10% test setting is disabled. Additional Trial loot rolls and native player-count NoDrop adjustments still apply.

Mythical bases are classified as Elite; saved item and unique IDs are unchanged. All 64 active variants explicitly enable native Chronicle collection; retired variants are excluded. Headhunter and all Screams of the Desiccated variants have original transparent skull/bone belt inventory artwork in HD and low-end resolutions.
