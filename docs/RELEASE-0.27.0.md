# Astral Workshop 0.27.0

- Astral Atlas grants one point per character level through 120. Reset restores the full level budget; the point display supports three digits.
- Mythical Uniques: 0.05% base chance per native Trial loot roll at Players 1 (1/2000). Additional loot rolls and native player-count NoDrop adjustments still apply. All 64 active variants are Elite bases and eligible for Chronicle collection; retired variants remain excluded.
- Headhunter, Mageblood and Screams of the Desiccated have original 2x1 belt icons. Mythical belts retain their native belt family and full 16-slot potion capacity. Potion-panel behavior still needs in-game confirmation.
- Mythical sky beams and glow are attached to dropped items only.
- Split Arrow total damage is halved, including elemental damage and all four split generations. Tooltip and character damage display match.
- Trial monster damage is halved. Burning Souls, Banished vampires and Stygian Doll Shamans join the pool with native skills and the existing Trial loot routes. Trial Temptresses use their ranged attack slot.
- Ctrl+J toggles the Trial modifier panel. A Trial map cannot be reopened in the same game.
- Material-stash hit areas and automatic sorting for Uber keys, Ancient statues and shards are corrected.
- Alpha's Call Ravens use their own skill lookup to preserve their attack budget. Avatar of the Wilds now describes its Golem transformation.
- Maghda's Domain/Labyrinth modifier clones use their ordinary counterparts' Hell resistances plus 15 percentage points, replacing stale blanket immunities.
- All eight Splash Charm upgrades toward Collin's Destruction use one ordinary key instead of a Gem Bag. Other ingredients are unchanged.

Validation: native combat and Astral tests, Mythical drop reachability, belt/caster data checks and character-sheet checks passed. In-game visual confirmation remains necessary for the belt panel and revised effects. The reported Dodge/Avoid tooltip-versus-stats discrepancy remains unresolved.

Restart the game after updating. The save profile remains ReimaginedWorkshop-v2. Existing rolled items retain their properties. No temporary Mythical test-drop setting is enabled.
